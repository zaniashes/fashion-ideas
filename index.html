Why these get saved and shared

Specific moments

Date night, wedding guest, church. People shop for an occasion, so they save the video.

Identity hooks

“Born to dress like this” makes viewers feel seen, and they tag friends.

Pain points

“Nothing to wear” names a problem the viewer already has.

Constraints

“One blazer, five ways” feels fresh even with basic clothes.
